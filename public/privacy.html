Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-19

Free Diff Checker compares text in your browser. We do not receive the text you compare for server-side processing. This policy covers advertising, analytics, and hosting.

Summary

Local comparison

Diffing runs in the page and a same-origin Web Worker. Compared text is not uploaded for comparison.

No accounts

There is no sign-up and no user profile on this site.

Ads

We use Adsterra banner and related formats. Ad partners may use cookies or similar technologies under their policies.

Analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics (privacy-oriented beacon) may collect aggregated usage metrics without third-party ad trackers from us.

Text and files you compare

Pasted text, merged results, and files you open or drop are handled in your browser. A same-origin worker may process that text locally. We do not operate a “share diff” server that stores your content.

Local storage

Your color theme preference may be stored under diffvoidcom_theme in local storage (inherited from the upstream engine). You can clear it in browser site settings.

Advertising (Adsterra)

Advertisements are served by Adsterra and its partners. They may process IP address, device data, and interaction signals. We do not sell your compared text to advertisers.

Hosting

The site is served via Cloudflare Workers static assets. Cloudflare may log standard request metadata (IP, path, user-agent, timestamps) for delivery and security.

Open source

The diff engine is based on MIT-licensed diffvoid by Timo Heimonen. You can inspect client-side source files under public/ in this repository.

Contact

See the contact page for feedback channels.
