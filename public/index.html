How it works

  1. 1
    Paste or openPaste one text into each panel, or drop or open a file. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded.
  2. 2
    CompareThe diff appears automatically, line by line. Use ignore whitespace to set aside indentation and spacing, and ignore case for upper and lower case.
  3. 3
    ReviewStep through the changes with the arrows, Alt+Up/Down or F7, or click the map on the right. changes only hides unchanged lines, and unified shows both texts in one column.
  4. 4
    Merge and exportSelect a change and keep its left or right version. Copy either side, or save the differences as a patch.

Reading the diff

hello there Modified changed characters in red
Added or missing an empty gap on the other side
moved(x); Moved lines in another place, edits in red
café Same characters different Unicode form (NFC/NFD)
ab Invisible hidden spaces and marks
pаypal Confusable lookalike letters, e.g. Cyrillic

Good to know

  • Copy L/R copies the exact original text. Clean L/R appears when invisible characters are found and copies a normalized version.
  • Drag the divider to resize the panels. Double-click it to reset to 50/50.
  • use left and use right change the text on the other side, and undo takes a merge back. copy diff and save .patch describe the exact texts, including differences that an ignore option hides.
  • Compared text is processed locally in your browser, not on our servers for diffing.
